Recipient Bios

Luisa K. Carter

2020 Graduate - Health Canada and Public Health Agency of Canada Science Management Development Program

Manager
Office of the Deputy Chief Science Officer
Science and Policy Integration Branch
Public Health Agency of Canada

Luisa is currently serving as a senior policy advisor in the Office of the Deputy Chief Science Officer of the Science and Policy Integration Branch at the Public Health Agency of Canada.

Luisa holds a Bachelor of Science in Biology and Graduate Diploma in Ecotoxicology, both from Concordia University. She was manager at the Consumer and Hazardous Product Safety Directorate (CHPSD) between 2002 and 2017, first as the Head of the Cosmetics Division overseeing all regulatory, scientific and policy aspects of cosmetics safety in Canada. In 2011, she moved to the International and Intergovernmental Affairs Division to manage CHPSD’s strategic engagement with international and domestic partners and took on the External Relations Division in 2016, in charge of consumer product safety publications, web presence and social media.

Luisa entered the Science Management Development Program (SMDP) in 2017. She credits the Program for giving her the opportunity to explore different parts of the Health Portfolio and understand the interconnections. Her first assignment was at the Public Health Agency of Canada in the Centre for Food-borne, Environmental and Zoonotic Infectious Diseases, working as the Associate Director and acting Director of the Policy Integration and Zoonoses Division, supporting interesting files such as Lyme disease, Prion diseases and the Infectious Disease and Climate Change Fund (a Grants & Contributions program). Her second assignment was as the Senior Manager of the Food and Drugs Act Substances Assessment Division at Health Canada’s Safe Environments Directorate, overseeing scientific risk assessment, regulations, research and policy related to new chemicals found in substances also regulated under the Food and Drugs Act. Her last assignment was as Senior Policy Advisor in PHAC’s Office of the Chief Officer supporting the Agency on interdepartmental science initiatives, where she has remained on a permanent basis, supporting science coordination for COVID-19 response during the pandemic, followed by leading the President’s Strengthening the Voice of Science initiative, a precursor to PHAC’s Science Strategy, and providing leadership on a number of horizontal science initiatives with the federal science community.

Philippe Bélanger

2023 Graduate - Health Canada and Public Health Agency of Canada Science Management Development Program

Response Readiness Unit Chief
Center of Emergency Response
Public Health Agency of Canada

Throughout the program, Philippe completed three leadership assignments that significantly contributed to his professional development.

As Senior Manager of the Public Service Occupational Health Program at Health, he led major modernization initiatives to enhance occupational health services, collaborated with central agencies on regulatory amendments, and contributed to strategic budget planning. His leadership and innovation in this role were recognized with a Health Canada Assistant Deputy Minister’s Award.

As Manager in the Emerging Sciences Division of the New Substances Assessment and Control Bureau, he oversaw scientific risk assessments under the Canadian Environmental Protection Act and managed three teams specializing in biotechnology and nanotechnology. In this capacity, he strengthened science-based decision-making, promoted interdepartmental collaboration, and contributed to a comprehensive regulatory policy review.

For his final assignment, as the Director of the Response Support and Technical Advice Division within the Centre for Emergency Response at the Public Health Agency of Canada, Philippe established a new division dedicated to enhancing emergency response capacity and providing technical advice during public health events. He led the Mobilization Framework Project, a cross-organizational initiative designed to embed a culture of readiness and responsiveness across the agency. This experience further developed his expertise in strategic planning, emergency coordination, stakeholder engagement, and organizational change, underscoring his ability to lead in complex and dynamic operational contexts.

Graduation from the Science Management Development Program represents a significant milestone in Mr. Bélanger’s career. The program has equipped him with the strategic insight, leadership acumen, and confidence to assume broader executive responsibilities within Canada’s public service. He remains deeply committed to advancing and promoting organizational excellence, and fostering a collaborative, adaptive, and innovative federal workforce dedicated to serving Canadians with professionalism and integrity.

Philippe is currently the Response Readiness Unit Chief within the Center of Emergency Response at PHAC.

Dr. Ming Su

2023 Graduate - Health Canada and Public Health Agency of Canada Science Management Development Program

Director of the Centre for Policy, Pediatrics and International Collaboration
Biologic and Radiopharmaceutical Drugs Directorate
Health Canada’s Health Products and Food Branch

Ming is a proud participant of HCSMDP in Cohort 24 (2020-2023). Being a trained scientist and with a subject matter expert in public health, she embraced stretch assignments in Health Canada and the Public Health Agency of Canada that challenged her in new ways.

Ming played an important role in Canada’s COVID-19 response, leading improvements to surveillance data systems through close collaboration with P/T partners and other government departments that supported a coordinated whole-of-government approach. Her leadership helped close critical evidence gaps and ensured timely, reliable data for decision-making.

Ming went on to establish a new division within PHAC focused on strengthening surveillance workforce competency. She built the structure and partnerships needed to enhance national expertise, working with international, academic, and internal collaborators to advance public health capacity. From 2022 to 2024, she led the integration of COVID-19 surveillance into Canada’s routine respiratory monitoring systems, modernizing the approach while responding to multiple outbreaks and urgent data needs.

Ming credits her growth during the 3-year assignments to a “village” of mentors, coaches, and colleagues who offered support and feedback throughout. With their support, Ming recognized, early in the HCSMDP journey, the importance of learning tools and strategies to integrate work with personal life - an essential foundation for taking on leadership challenges with resilience. Her first assignment reflected this insight, focusing on mental wellness and establishing sustainable habits to support both personal and professional growth.

Ming graduates from HCSMDP as a more self-aware and resilient leader, committed to fostering excellence in public health and supporting others in their growth. She continues to champion innovation, wellness, and collaboration in her current role at Health Canada.

Dr. Cynthia Colapinto

2024 Graduate - Health Canada and Public Health Agency of Canada Science Management Development Program

Director of the Policy and Data Division
Health Product Shortages Directorate
Health Product and Food Branch
Health Canada

Dr. Cynthia Colapinto is the Director of the Policy and Data Division in Health Canada’s Health Product Shortages Directorate, where she is responsible for science-based policy development, collaborative stakeholder engagement, and innovative, data-driven solutions to prevent and mitigate critical, national drug and medical device shortages.

A registered dietitian with a PhD in Population Health from the University of Ottawa, she began her career in public health nutrition and maternal-child health research, grounding her work in epidemiology and population health.

Since joining Health Canada in 2014, Cynthia has held progressively senior leadership roles advancing evidence-based policy, operational transformation and regulatory oversight. Through the Science Management Development Program, she served as A/Associate Director in the Health Product Compliance Directorate, leading key transformation initiatives under the mentorship of Linsey Hollett and Dr. Kim Godard. She then served for two years as A/Associate Director General in the Biologic and Radiopharmaceutical Drugs Directorate, working alongside Dr. Celia Lourenco, and then Sophie Sommerer, to oversee regulatory review of clinical trials and biological products. She was an active member of the Branch Executive Committee and led key activities, including the directorate operational review, strategic human resources management, communications for high-profile drugs, and the development of equitable guidance for sperm donation for men who have sex with men.

Cynthia is grateful for the unwavering support of her mentors, colleagues, and fellow MDP alumni, whose guidance and encouragement have been instrumental to her continued development as a public sector leader.

Stephanie Hardy

2024 Graduate - Health Canada and Public Health Agency of Canada Science Management Development Program

Director of Cannabis Inspection Operations
Cannabis, Tobacco and Controlled Substances Directorate
Regulatory Operations and Enforcement Branch
Health Canada

Stephanie Hardy holds a bachelor's degree of science in toxicology (University of Guelph) and a master's degree of public health (University of Waterloo). She began her career in the medical devices industry before joining Health Canada’s Health Products and Food Branch (HPFB) where she led a science policy unit focused on regulatory policy and guidance for the authorization of biologic drugs, including biosimilars. In this capacity, she also played a key role in international regulatory collaboration and global regulatory strengthening initiatives. A highlight of her early career was a secondment to the World Health Organization (WHO), where she supported the Blood Regulators Network and contributed to the development of global guidance on pandemic vaccines (H5N1/H1N1).

Stephanie was accepted into the Science Management Development Program (SMDP) in 2019 and began her first assignment as Associate Director General in BRDD, which placed her at the heart of the COVID-19 response. This assignment provided invaluable opportunities to strengthen all leadership competencies—particularly promoting innovation, guiding change, and mobilizing multidisciplinary teams to facilitate timely approval of COVID vaccines and biologic treatments. The unprecedented collaboration with other regulators, the WHO, other departments and industry during this period and contributing to the response to this public health emergency remains a defining achievement in her career. Then, she joined Health Canada's Regulatory Operations and Enforcement Branch (ROEB) where she led several strategic initiatives, including the division’s Build Back Better modernization efforts and the implementation of the new national Natural Health Product Good Manufacturing Practices (NHP GMP) inspection program. Her assignments with ROEB helped her in gaining valuable experience in operational program management, compliance and enforcement and stakeholder engagement.

Finally, in 2023-2024, Stephanie served as Acting Director of the Health Product Compliance and Risk Management Division, overseeing a large and diverse division responsible for compliance verification and enforcement activities for health products, border compliance policy, data analytics, and the NHP GMP and Good Pharmacovigilance Practices inspection programs. This position contributed to strengthening her competencies, especially in mobilizing a large national program, extensive internal and external stakeholder engagement, and directly overseeing a complex national program budget.

In November 2024, Stephanie was appointed Director of Cannabis Inspection Operations in ROEB’s within the newly formed Cannabis, Tobacco and Controlled Substances Directorate. Stephanie mostly credits the SMDP and her assignment hosts for expanding her leadership capabilities, exposing her to diverse challenges, and providing a network of peers and mentors who have supported her growth.
